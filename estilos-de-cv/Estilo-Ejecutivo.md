**Estilo Ejecutivo / Executive Resume**

*El currículum del C-suite: P\&L ownership, board experience, strategic vision, 2 páginas con peso institucional*

# **Industrias donde funciona mejor**

El Executive Resume es el formato esperado en virtualmente todos los sectores corporativos tradicionales y financieros para roles senior. Su uso decrece o adapta solo en contextos específicos: tech ultra-modern (founders), academia, sector creativo puro.

### **Industrias de alta compatibilidad**

* **Industria y manufactura grande**

* **Energy y utilities**

* **Banking tradicional y wealth management**

* **Insurance y reinsurance**

* **Pharma y healthcare**

* **Consumer goods grandes**

* **Private Equity y Venture Capital senior**

* **Strategic consulting top-tier**.

### **Compatibilidad media**

* **Tech BigTech mature (CEO/CFO levels)**

* **Tech scale-ups maduros (Series E+, pre-IPO)**

* **Real estate y construction grandes**

* **Telecom y media**

* **Logistics y transportation**

# **Estructura exacta del CV**

La estructura Executive es codificada y poco variable. Cabecera con name \+ title \+ contact. Executive Summary o Career Profile prominente (4-7 líneas en bloque o bullets). Professional Experience cronológica inversa con descripción narrativa \+ bulleted achievements. Education compacta. Board Memberships, Speaking, Awards, Publications según relevancia.

### **Orden canónico de secciones**

1. 1\. Header: Name (prominent), current title or target title, location (City, State/Country), email, phone, LinkedIn URL. Sin foto en USA/UK; opcional discreta en Europa continental tradicional para perfiles muy senior.

2. 2\. Executive Summary / Career Profile: 4-7 líneas o 3-5 bullets que capturen positioning. Ejemplo: 'Multinational CEO with 22 years driving transformation in industrial sector. Track record of growing revenue from $800M to $3.2B and expanding EBITDA margin from 8% to 18% across two public companies. Board director at two NYSE-listed firms.'

3. 3\. Areas of Expertise / Core Competencies: 8-15 keywords en formato 3-column grid. P\&L Management · M\&A · Digital Transformation · Board Governance · Crisis Leadership · International Expansion · etc.

4. 4\. Professional Experience: cronología inversa, cada role con company / title / dates / location. Brief 1-2 line context describiendo company size y mandate. 4-7 bullets de achievements quantified.

5. 5\. Earlier Career (cuando \>20 años de experiencia): paragraph compacto con roles más antiguos sin detalle bullet, solo company / role / dates.

6. 6\. Education: degrees con institutions y dates. Honors si destacables. Executive education (HBS AMP, Stanford SEP, INSEAD AVIRA, IMD OWP) prominente para perfiles ejecutivos sin MBA tradicional.

7. 7\. Board Memberships: current y past public boards, private boards notables, advisory boards, non-profit boards. Each with role (Director, Chair, Audit Committee Chair) y dates.

8. 8\. Speaking & Publications (opcional pero alta señal): keynote conferences (Davos, Milken, Aspen, Sun Valley), publications en HBR/MIT Sloan/WSJ/FT, books authored.

9. 9\. Awards & Recognition (opcional): industry awards (CEO of the Year, Best CFO en industry rankings), Forbes 50 Most Powerful Women, Time 100, etc.

10. 10\. Languages, Citizenships (en perfiles internacionales)

### **Jerarquía visual**

Jerarquía visual prioriza Executive Summary \> Professional Experience \> Board Memberships. Education es subordinada (excepto MBA top-tier que se lista pero sin extensa elaboración). Areas of Expertise sirve como keyword density section para search consultant scanning.

### **Longitud y densidad**

2 páginas estándar inquebrantable.

Una página: percibido como insuficiente para senior executive con 20+ años.

Tres páginas: percibido como verboso, pobre judgment editorial. Solo aceptable con apéndice de Board/Speaking detallado para perfiles altamente external-facing.

Variante Long-form (4-6 páginas) existe para perfiles académicos transitioning a board, ex-government con extensive policy work, o para searches que explícitamente requieren detail (rare).

# **Secciones obligatorias, opcionales y prohibidas**

### **Obligatorias**

* **Executive Summary / Career Profile:** 4-7 líneas que capturan total years experience, scope (industries, geographies, P\&L size), top 3-4 achievements signature, current/target positioning. La sección más leída del documento.

* **Areas of Expertise / Core Competencies:** 8-15 keywords en grid format. Mix de functional capabilities (P\&L, M\&A, Digital Transformation) y industry/contextual (Pharma Regulatory, Asia Expansion, Turnaround Leadership). Sirve para keyword scanning por search consultants y ATS.

* **Professional Experience con achievements quantified:** Cada role con company name \+ role \+ dates \+ location. Brief 1-2 line context. 4-7 bullets achievement-oriented con métricas: revenue growth, EBITDA expansion, market share gains, deals closed (M\&A value), team scaled, geographies entered.

* **Education:** Undergraduate \+ MBA o equivalent advanced degree \+ executive education programs. Prominent presentation for top-tier institutions (HBS, GSB, Wharton, INSEAD, LBS, MIT Sloan, IMD).

* **Board Memberships (cuando relevant):** Current public board roles, past public boards, private notable boards, advisory boards, non-profit governance. Critical signal de institutional credibility.

### **Opcionales (según contexto)**

* **Speaking & Publications:** Keynote presentations en Davos, Aspen Ideas, Milken Global, Sun Valley, industry conferences. HBR, MIT Sloan Management Review, WSJ Op-eds, FT contributions, books authored.

* **Awards & Recognition:** Industry-specific (Banker of the Year, CEO of the Year), broader (Forbes lists, Time 100, Fortune Most Powerful), academic recognitions.

* **Professional Memberships:** Business Roundtable, World Economic Forum, Conference Board, Aspen Institute, Council on Foreign Relations, Bilderberg Group attendees, Trilateral Commission.

* **Languages:** Especially relevant para EU-based, multinational profiles. Native, Fluent, Professional Working, Limited niveles tradicionales.

* **Citizenship / Work Authorization:** Para perfiles globales: dual citizenships notables, work authorization across major markets.

* **Civic Leadership:** Trustee de major institutions (museums, universities, hospitals), philanthropy roles, government advisory positions (sin partisan affiliation salvo career relevance).

* **Earlier Career compacto:** Para experiencia \>20 años, paragraph cubriendo roles older sin bullets detallados, solo company/role/dates.

* **Personal Interests (controversial, decreasing):** Práctica anglosajona tradicional incluía 'Marathon runner, classical pianist'. Decreasing en favor de gravitas focus, pero aún acceptable en algunos contexts conservadores europeos.

### **Prohibidas o desaconsejadas**

* **Photo en USA/UK/CA/AU:** Mismas razones legales que cualquier otro estilo americano. Inaceptable.

* **Skills bars o competency ratings visuales:** Inmediatamente descalifica como junior-amateur.

* **Iconography decorativa:** Email icon, phone icon, location pin: aceptables si extremely subtle. Iconography más allá: descalifica.

* **Soft skills genéricos sin substantiation:** 'Strategic thinker', 'visionary leader' standalone sin evidence concreta es padding fluff. Inadequate para C-suite scrutiny.

* **Listing all jobs ever held:** Earlier Career section condenses pre-15-years experience. No need bullet detail para roles de hace 25 años.

* **Summer internships, college achievements (excepto extraordinary):** Irrelevant. Eliminar.

* **Personal social media handles (excluyendo LinkedIn):** Twitter/X, Instagram, personal Facebook irrelevant. LinkedIn URL standard.

* **Hobbies sin gravitas:** 'Enjoys spending time with family, traveling' es waste. Si mencionar interests, debe transmitir character (Trustee de museum, marathon runner que califica para Boston, certified sommelier).

* **Acronyms unexplained:** Internal company acronyms (e.g. 'Led DPSU initiative') sin explanation pierden señal. Spell out o eliminate.

* **Political affiliations (excepto si career-relevant como ex-government):** Risk de polarization. Avoid salvo cuando es part of career narrative (Cabinet Secretary transitioning post-government).

# **Características visuales y diseño**

Visualmente el Executive Resume es deliberadamente sobrio, autoritario, pre-digital en estética. Inspirado en formatos de white-shoe law firms (Cravath, Wachtell), white-shoe consulting (McKinsey legacy), y banking M\&A pitch books. Tipografía conservative or modern serif/sans-serif premium, paleta cromática extremely restrained, márgenes generosos, alineaciones precise.

### **Layout y columnas**

Una columna principal o 2-column con sidebar muy mínimo (solo Areas of Expertise grid en algunos templates)

Headers de sección prominent pero contained, frecuentemente con horizontal rule sutil debajo

Cabecera centrada o left-aligned con name dominant

Bullets con indentación clara, hanging indent, leading consistent

Frecuent uso de partial bold para emphasis en metrics: 'Increased revenue from $800M to \*\*$3.2B\*\*'

### **Espaciado, márgenes y whitespace**

Interlineado 1.1-1.2

Espaciado entre sections 12-16pt

Espaciado entre roles 8-12pt

Márgenes 0.7-1.0 inch (US Letter) o 2-2.5cm (A4 europeo)

### **Iconografía y elementos gráficos**

Sin iconos decorativos. Maximum: tiny mail/phone/LinkedIn símbolos en cabecera, all en mismo color sutil, monocromáticos.

Algunos templates moderate Executive incluyen iconos minimalistas de cabecera; templates más conservadores omiten completamente.

### **Densidad de información y balance visual**

Densidad informativa moderada-alta. 2 páginas son suficientes solo si cada palabra tiene weight.

Whitespace funcional para gravitas: el resume no debe sentirse abigarrado.

Cada bullet debe tener achievement substantive; padding bullets descalifican.

# **Sistema tipográfico**

La tipografía Executive Resume tiene dos escuelas: (1) Classical Serif (Times New Roman histórico, Georgia, Garamond, Cambria, Minion, Sabon) que comunica tradición, gravitas, white-shoe heritage; (2) Modern Sans (Helvetica, Calibri, Frutiger, Avenir, Inter, Source Sans) que comunica modernidad sin sacrificar seriedad.

### **Tipografías recomendadas**

* **Garamond / Adobe Garamond Pro:** Serif clásica humanista. Comunica heritage corporate y editorial. Cuerpo 10.5-11.5pt, headers 12-14pt, name 18-22pt. Especialmente apropiada para industrias tradicionales (finance legacy, law-adjacent corporate, family business).

* **Sabon:** Serif highly refined. Diseñada por Jan Tschichold. Usada en muchos publishers premium. Para perfiles que quieren signal sofisticación editorial.

* **Georgia:** Serif de Microsoft optimizada para pantalla. Más legible que Times en small sizes. Ampliamente disponible. Cuerpo 10.5-11.5pt.

* **Cambria:** Microsoft serif moderna optimizada para print y screen. ATS-safe. Default conservadora segura.

* **Times New Roman / Times:** Most traditional. Some perceive as outdated (especially en USA tech-adjacent contexts). Aún acceptable para industrias most conservative (banking, law). Avoid en Modern Executive en general.

* **Calibri:** Sans-serif Microsoft default. ATS-safest sans-serif. Aceptable para Executive moderno.

* **Helvetica Neue / Helvetica:** Sans-serif clásica modernist. Premium feel. Necesita embedded en PDF para garantizar rendering.

* **Avenir / Avenir Next:** Sans-serif geométrica humanizada. Premium feel. Apropiada para Executive moderno con elegance.

* **Frutiger / Univers:** Swiss design heritage. Premium sans-serif. Frequently usadas en corporate identity de top firms.

### **Jerarquía de tamaños**

Name: 20-26pt

Section headers: 12-14pt en bold o small caps

Job titles: 11-12pt bold

Company name: 11-12pt regular o italic

Body bullets: 10.5-11.5pt

Dates / metadata: 10-11pt regular o italic

### **Tipografías a evitar**

Comic Sans, Papyrus, Brush Script: completamente descalificantes

Light weights (\<400) o display fonts en body: ilegibles, no apropiadas

Custom fonts no embedded: PDF debe garantizar rendering correcto

Mezcla de \>2 familias: máximo serif para headers \+ sans para body, o viceversa, o solo una familia con weights diferentes

# **Uso del color**

Color en Executive Resume es extremadamente restrained. Negro/charcoal para texto principal. Acento opcional en navy, burgundy, dark forest green, o gold/bronze para name y headers. Nunca colores brillantes, gradients, multi-colores.

### **Paletas recomendadas**

* **Pure traditional (charcoal/navy):** Charcoal \#1A1A1A or \#2C2C2C para body. Navy \#1F3A5F para name y section headers. La paleta más conservative, apropiada para banking, law, traditional consulting.

* **Charcoal \+ burgundy:** Burgundy \#722F37 o Bordeaux \#8B0A1A para name y accent. Comunica heritage corporate, law-adjacent, sectors tradicionales europeos.

* **Charcoal \+ dark forest:** Dark Forest Green \#1F4631 para acento. Apropiada para family business, sustainability-focused executives, certain European industries.

* **Charcoal \+ gold/bronze:** Gold \#B8860B o Bronze \#8B6914 para acento. Premium feel, luxury industries (LVMH, Hermès C-suite), private banking, family offices.

* **Pure monochrome:** Solo charcoal/black \+ grays. Maximally austere, apropiado para legal contexts, ultra-traditional banking.

* **Modern executive (charcoal \+ tech blue):** Charcoal \+ medium blue \#2E75B6. Apropiado para tech-adjacent executives, modern industries.

# **Estilo de redacción**

Writing Executive es authoritative, formal, results-focused. Verbos en pasado simple para roles previos, presente para current. Tono medido sin understated. Métricas precise pero contextualizadas. Achievements framed strategically.

### **Tono y voz**

Tono ejecutivo: confident sin arrogance, factual sin coldness, strategic sin abstraction.

Voz tercera persona implícita (verbos en pasado): 'Led $1.2B turnaround', 'Spearheaded acquisition of X'. Algunos templates usan first-person Executive Summary; both acceptable.

Avoid: jargon excessive, acronyms unexplained, business-school clichés ('synergies leveraged', 'paradigms shifted'), self-deprecation, hedging.

### **Verbos de acción y estructura de frases**

Strategic leadership: Led, Spearheaded, Orchestrated, Drove, Directed, Championed, Architected, Established

Growth/Performance: Grew, Expanded, Increased, Doubled, Tripled, Achieved, Delivered, Generated

Transformation: Transformed, Repositioned, Restructured, Reengineered, Turned around, Revitalized, Pivoted

M\&A/Capital: Executed, Closed, Negotiated, Structured, Raised, Divested, Integrated, Consummated

Governance: Chaired, Sat on, Advised, Guided, Counseled, Reported to (Board)

Avoid: Helped with, Worked on, Was involved in, Participated in (passive, weak)

### **Uso de métricas, KPIs y storytelling**

Revenue: 'Grew revenue from $1.4B to $3.8B (+170% over 5 years)'

EBITDA: 'Expanded EBITDA margin from 12% to 22%, generating $480M incremental EBITDA'

Headcount: 'Scaled organization from 1,200 to 4,500 across 14 countries'

M\&A: 'Executed 7 strategic acquisitions totaling $2.8B in transaction value'

Capital raised: 'Led IPO raising $620M at $3.4B valuation'

Cost: 'Delivered $340M annual run-rate savings through operational restructuring'

Market: 'Increased market share from 12% to 28%, becoming \#2 player by 2024'

TSR: 'Delivered total shareholder return of 247% over 6-year tenure vs S\&P 500 \+89%'

### **Diferencias culturales**

USA: tone descrito arriba

UK: ligeramente más understated, evita superlatives como 'transformative', prefiere 'substantial', 'material'

Europa continental: aún más sobrio, descripción factual prima

Asia: respeto por institutional hierarchy enfatizado; mention of reporting structure prominent

Latin America: frequently bilingual (EN/ES o EN/PT), tone professional español/portugués corporate

# **Compatibilidad ATS**

Executive Resume tiene compatibilidad ATS variable. Bien construido (single column, semantic structure, standard sections) parsea well en Workday, SuccessFactors. Templates over-designed con tablas y multi-column reducen parsing significantly.

### **Score ATS estimado**

### **Elementos que mejoran el parsing**

* Single column layout o 2-column con sidebar mínimo simple

* Standard section headers (Professional Experience, Education, Board Memberships)

* Spell out company names completos (Goldman Sachs, no GS; JPMorgan Chase, no JPM en first mention)

* Acronyms with explanation primera mención

* Métricas en formato standard ($1.2B, no 1.2 Bn or 1,200M)

* Dates formato consistente (MM/YYYY o Month YYYY)

* PDF generado from text source (Word/InDesign), no scanned

* Avoid: tablas complex, headers/footers con info esencial, image-based logos, custom fonts no embedded

### **Elementos que rompen ATS**

* Multi-column layouts decorativos

* Image-based name banner

* Tables para Areas of Expertise (preferir text grid)

* Custom fonts no embedded

* Photo embedded en USA aplicaciones (descalifica \+ reduce parsing area)
